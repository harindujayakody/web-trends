Clear answers

Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Write to us and we’ll help.

What types of charities can I donate to, and how do I find them?

Browse campaigns by health, education, disaster relief and food security. Every listed fundraiser includes a clear purpose, goal and progress update.

Is my donation tax-deductible, and how do I claim it?

Eligibility depends on your country and the receiving organization. We provide a receipt and the charity’s details so you can check with a local tax adviser.

Can I donate anonymously?

Yes. You may hide your name from the public supporter list while the charity still receives your contribution.

What percentage of my donation reaches the charity?

Each campaign shows its processing and administration details before you donate, so there are no surprises.

Can I donate goods or services instead of money?

Selected campaigns accept practical support. Look for an in-kind donation note on the campaign page or contact the organizer.
